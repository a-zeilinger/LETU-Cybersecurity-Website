What we do

Practical skills, one topic at a time

Meetings are designed to give students a place to practice, ask questions, and build experience outside the classroom.

Practice Linux, scanning, network traffic analysis, digital forensics, password security, cryptography, log analysis, OSINT, and web security.

CTF Practice

Apply what you learn in Capture the Flag challenges and team practice sessions, including National Cyber League-style challenges.

Competitions & Events

Explore cybersecurity competitions, conferences, and other opportunities that club members may attend together.
